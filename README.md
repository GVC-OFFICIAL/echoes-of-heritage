# 🗺️ Echoes of Heritage

**A map of Ungheni you can touch.**

I'm building an interactive board that brings the history of Ungheni to life.

It's a map of the town and the area around it, with hidden touch points on its landmarks. Touch a spot, like the Eiffel Bridge, an old church or a village, and the board answers. A speaker plays the story of the place, and the screen shows old photos and facts about it.

## How it works

- 🔹 Copper tape under the map turns each landmark into a touch button
- 🔹 A capacitive touch sensor (MPR121) notices your finger, with up to 12 places per board
- 🔹 A Raspberry Pi plays the audio and shows the pictures on the screen
- 🔹 A small amplifier (PAM8403) and speaker play the stories

## Hardware

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Raspberry Pi 4 | Runs the app, plays audio and images |
| MPR121 (12 channels) | Capacitive touch sensing |
| Copper tape | Touch points under the map |
| 7" HDMI screen | Photos and facts |
| PAM8403 amplifier + speaker | Audio stories |
| 5V power supply | Powers everything |

## Why

Our local history usually stays inside books and museums. I want people, especially kids, to *touch* it and hear it in a way they'll remember.
